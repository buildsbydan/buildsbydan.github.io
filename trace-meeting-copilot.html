The 10-second version
When your mind blanks mid-meeting, pick ONE letter and scan for what's missing.
Target — what problem, for whom  ·  Risks — blast radius, failure modes  ·  Assumptions — what's unverified  ·  Constraints — cost, ownership, rollback  ·  Evidence — detection, validation, metrics.

Presenters prepare for T, R, C. They rarely prepare for A and E. Aim there.
